History

Here's a brief history of the property. I'm working on getting more.

Year Event
1966 Original developer founded Applewood Hill Homes Association: Regency Hill (Floyd W. McBurney Sr., President)
1972 Architect John Bruni designs the house; blueprints show the owners to be Mr. and Mrs. Peter O Johnson
1974? Construction completed
1983 Peter O. Johnson and Martha B. Johnson apply for a permit for private sewage and are the owners at this time
1988 Applewood covenants are renewed for another 24 years. (The end of the first term was 1990). At this time, the owners of Lot 2 (i.e. 7111) are Anthony Merliss and Ellen Merliss.
~1992 Purchased by Weldon and Caroln Shelp
2014 Sold to Chad E Fickett by Weldon D Shelp and Carolyn H Shelp on 01/06/2014
2022 Sold to Melissa M Papineau and William E Papineau by Chad E Fickett on 05/06/2022
2025 Sold to Mark R Riordan and Tamara J Riordan by Melissa M Papineau and William E Papineau on 07/23/2025

Thanks to Jeff Drengler for his assistance in compiling this history.
